# Demo flow

1. Sign in to the PayLeak dashboard.
2. Connect a PayPal account (use a sandbox account for demonstrations).
3. Review invoices and payment activity.
4. Open leak analysis to explain observed fees.
5. Compare available payment routes and review the quote assumptions.
6. Ask the AI assistant to explain selected payment data.
7. Review reminders and notification settings.

Use clearly labeled sample data unless the demonstration has explicit approval to
use real account information. Do not expose credentials or payment secrets on screen.
